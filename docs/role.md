# The role at a glance

## In one sentence

A forward deployed engineer works close to customers to understand a real operational problem, build or adapt software that solves it in the customer's environment, and carry deployment lessons back into the product.

That summary is a synthesis of the employer postings below, not a formal industry standard. Palantir describes engineers working alongside customers, building custom applications and owning delivery from the first conversation through shipping; Pace and Clarion describe discovery, implementation, launch, iteration, and product feedback; Revel describes integrations with physical systems and field troubleshooting.

## The delivery loop

| Step | What the engineer does |
| --- | --- |
| Discover | Observe workflows; talk with users and technical stakeholders; clarify the outcome and constraints. |
| Shape | Turn an ambiguous need into a scoped technical plan, prototype, integration, or workflow. |
| Build | Write production code, connect APIs/data/systems, test behavior, and address security or reliability needs. |
| Deploy | Coordinate access, configuration, release, monitoring, and user onboarding in a live environment. |
| Improve | Debug real usage, iterate with users, and package recurring solutions into reusable product capabilities. |

## What a week can include

- Customer discovery or workflow mapping
- Coding features, integrations, data pipelines, dashboards, or AI-enabled workflows
- Debugging deployment issues with customer and internal teams
- Testing, monitoring, documentation, and training
- Feeding repeated customer needs to product and engineering

The mix varies. Some teams build beside customers in production; others spend meaningful time in discovery or pre-sales. Physical-world roles can include field testing and travel. See [role variations](/full-time#how-the-role-varies) before comparing job titles.

## Questions to ask when evaluating a posting

- How much time is spent coding, with customers, in pre-sales, and traveling?
- Does the FDE own production launch and support, or hand off after implementation?
- What is custom per customer, and what is expected to become reusable product?
- Which systems are involved: cloud software, enterprise data, AI workflows, hardware, or edge infrastructure?
- What does success look like after 30, 90, and 180 days?
- How are security, customer data, and on-call expectations handled?

## FDE is a family of roles

Job titles overlap with customer engineering, implementation engineering, solutions engineering, field engineering, and technical consulting. The postings reviewed here are most engineering-heavy when they explicitly ask for production code ownership, integrations, deployment, debugging, or product contributions. Read the responsibilities rather than relying on the title.
