# Method & limits

## Research approach

- Reviewed public employer job descriptions found through searches for forward deployed engineer/software engineer internships and full-time roles.
- Prioritized primary employer-hosted postings, including Lever and Ashby job pages.
- Selected examples across software/data, AI workflow, robotics, and hardware deployment.
- Compared duties and qualifications, then summarized recurring patterns. The role overview and skill synthesis are this site's interpretation, not an official occupational standard.

## Snapshot and freshness

Research snapshot: **12 November 2026**. Job pages are dynamic: titles, eligibility, location, travel, and availability may change without notice. Always confirm the current information on the employer's own page before applying.

## Limits

- The sample is illustrative, not representative of every employer or geography.
- Some job boards render incomplete pages outside their interactive site; where detail was available in the employer listing, the linked source remains the authority.
- A posting describes one team's current needs. Requirements such as degree, years of experience, language, work authorization, and travel are not universal FDE requirements.
- “Forward deployed engineer” is not a standardized title. Similar jobs may be labeled customer engineer, deployment engineer, field engineer, or solutions engineer.
- This is career research, not a guarantee of hiring outcomes or a substitute for reading each live job description.
