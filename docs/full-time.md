# Full-time FDE roles

## What changes after an internship

Full-time jobs more often ask one engineer to own the delivery outcome across discovery, architecture, integration, launch, and production iteration. Some still hire early-career candidates; others require years of production experience. Check the actual seniority and scope rather than assuming the title sets a standard.

## How the role varies

### Embedded product engineering
Build features and integrations directly in the core codebase; customer problems guide product direction. Examples: Palantir, Pace, Clarion.

### Enterprise solution delivery
Map workflows, reconcile customer data, prototype and deploy solutions; convert repeat patterns to product. This can include pre-sales work, as Clarasight explicitly describes.

### Physical systems and field engineering
Integrate hardware, software, sensors, and edge infrastructure; troubleshoot in customer environments. Expect travel and domain-specific constraints, as in Clera’s robotics role.

## Role-shape checks

Before accepting a title, clarify the balance of production coding, configuration, pre-sales, implementation, support, and travel. These postings demonstrate that “FDE” can name meaningfully different jobs.
