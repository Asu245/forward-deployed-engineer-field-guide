## Quick read

An FDE applies software engineering inside a customer's real operating environment. The work often runs from discovery and integration through production launch, with customer feedback flowing back into the product. The title is not standardized: some jobs focus on AI workflows and enterprise data; others involve hardware, robotics, or edge systems.
